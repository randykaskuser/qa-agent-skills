# Example Output: The Creative Director Process

*When a user requests a script, this is the exact flow you should output.*

**User Prompt:** "I need a 45s script for an AI Resume Builder. Audience is frustrated job seekers."

---

## 1. Story Engine Selection
**Selected Framework:** The Pain / Enemy / Mechanism
**Reasoning:** Job seekers are experiencing acute pain (rejection). We need to validate that pain (Pain), blame the ATS bots (Enemy), and introduce the AI rewriter (Mechanism) as the savior.

---

## 2. Hook Generation & Scoring

**Candidate 1 (Curiosity):** "I found out why your resume is getting ignored."
- C: 7, S: 5, R: 8, SR: 4 -> Score: 16

**Candidate 2 (Contrarian/Fear):** "Stop applying to jobs online right now."
- C: 8, S: 8, R: 7, SR: 3 -> Score: 20

**Candidate 3 (Visual/Shock):** [Visual of dropping resumes in shredder] "No human is ever going to read your resume."
- C: 9, S: 9, R: 9, SR: 2 -> Score: 25

**Selection:** Candidate 3 wins (Score: 25). It's highly visual, shocking, and speaks to the core fear of the audience.

---

## 3. Creative Director Review (Self-Critique)

**Hook:** 9/10 - Candidate 3 is strong. Visual destruction is a great pattern interrupt.
**Retention:** 7/10 - Scene 3 was a bit static. *Correction: Added a snap zoom and a UI overlay pop to enforce the 2.5s cinematic rule.*
**Visual:** 8/10 - Google Flow tags were good, but I updated the lighting in Scene 2 to "Dark room, screen glow" to emphasize the depression of job hunting.
**Voice:** 9/10 - All lines under 10 words. Tones shift from 'Authority' to 'Empath' to 'Excitable Discoverer'.
**CTA:** 9/10 - Low friction ("Scan for free").
