# Example Script: Software (B2C / Prosumer)

**Product**: AI-Powered Resume Builder
**Audience**: Job seekers frustrated by constant rejections
**Duration**: 45s

---

## Scene 1 — Pattern Interrupt (0-4s)

**Goal**: Stop the scroll by revealing a harsh truth about their current strategy.
**Emotion**: Shock / Anxiety.
**Viewer Thought**: "Is my resume getting thrown out instantly?"

**Visual**:
- Camera: High angle looking down at a desk.
- Expression: Unseen.
- Action: A hand drops a stack of printed resumes into a shredder.
- Overlay: Big bold text: "NO HUMAN IS READING YOUR RESUME."
- Transition: Whip pan up to actor's face.

**Voiceover**:
Stop applying to hundreds of jobs online.
No human is actually reading your resume.

---

## Scene 2 — The Call Out & Enemy (4-12s)

**Goal**: Agitate the pain of the "black hole" job search and explain the ATS system.
**Emotion**: Frustration / Epiphany.
**Viewer Thought**: "That explains why I never hear back."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Over the shoulder (OTS). Darker room.
- Expression: Defeated posture.
- Action: Actor clicking "Submit Application" on a laptop screen. An instant "Automated Rejection" email pops up.
- Overlay: Red text: "Rejected by ATS."
- Transition: Fast snap zoom back to actor.

**Voiceover**:
You spend hours tweaking your bullet points.
Just to get an automated rejection email the next morning.
Because a robot scanned your resume and failed it.

---

## Scene 3 — Mechanism Reveal (12-22s)

**Goal**: Introduce the software as the key to beating the robot.
**Emotion**: Hope.
**Viewer Thought**: "I need this tool to get past the filter."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Screen recording push-in.
- Expression: N/A.
- Action: User pastes a job description URL into the software. The software instantly rewrites the resume bullet points to match the keywords perfectly.
- Overlay: Green checkmark popping up: "ATS Match: 98%".
- Transition: Cut back to actor, smiling confidently.

**Voiceover**:
You have to beat the robot first.
I use an AI tool that scans the exact job description.
And instantly rewrites my resume to bypass the filters.

---

## Scene 4 — Proof (22-30s)

**Goal**: Show the tangible result (interviews, not just a good resume).
**Emotion**: Desire.
**Viewer Thought**: "I want my inbox to look like that."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, handheld.
- Expression: Excited.
- Action: Actor holding up their phone to the camera, showing their email inbox.
- Overlay: Three email popups: "Interview Invitation", "Next Steps", "Phone Screen Scheduled".
- Transition: Match cut to actor at desk.

**Voiceover**:
My resume score went from forty percent to ninety-nine.
And I landed three interviews in one week.
For roles I actually wanted.

---

## Scene 5 — Future Pacing & CTA (30-40s)

**Goal**: Drive immediate action by removing friction.
**Emotion**: Urgency.
**Viewer Thought**: "I'm updating my resume today."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot. Clean, professional lighting.
- Expression: Direct, encouraging.
- Action: Pointing down to the link area.
- Overlay: UI Graphic: "Scan Your Resume (Free)".
- Transition: Fade to black.

**Voiceover**:
Stop sending your resume into a black hole.
Click the link to scan your resume for free.
And see why you're getting rejected.
