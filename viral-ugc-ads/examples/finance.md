# Example Script: Finance / Fintech App

**Product**: Automated High-Yield Savings & Investment App
**Audience**: Young professionals whose money is sitting in a traditional bank
**Duration**: 45s

---

## Scene 1 — Pattern Interrupt (0-4s)

**Goal**: Stop the scroll by showing them they are actively losing money.
**Emotion**: Alarm / Curiosity.
**Viewer Thought**: "Wait, my savings account is bad?"

**Visual**:
- Camera: Extreme Close Up (ECU) of a traditional debit card being cut in half with scissors.
- Expression: Unseen.
- Action: Scissors snapping through plastic.
- Overlay: Bold red text: "YOUR BANK IS SCAMMING YOU."
- Transition: Fast snap zoom out to actor holding the pieces.

**Voiceover**:
If your money is sitting in a normal savings account...
You are actively losing money every single day.

---

## Scene 2 — The Call Out & Enemy (4-12s)

**Goal**: Explain inflation vs. traditional bank interest rates simply.
**Emotion**: Anger / Betrayal.
**Viewer Thought**: "My bank only pays me pennies."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, handheld.
- Expression: Serious, explaining.
- Action: Holding up a phone showing a bank statement with "$0.12 Interest Earned".
- Overlay: Text bubble: "0.01% Interest Rate".
- Transition: Whip pan right.

**Voiceover**:
Your bank pays you point zero-one percent interest.
While inflation is sitting at four percent.
They take your money, lend it out, and keep the profit.

---

## Scene 3 — Mechanism Reveal (12-22s)

**Goal**: Introduce the fintech app as the effortless, smart alternative.
**Emotion**: Relief / Hope.
**Viewer Thought**: "How do I get the higher rate?"

**Visual**:
- Camera: Screen push-in.
- Expression: N/A.
- Action: Opening a clean, modern green-themed finance app. Moving money from "Checking" to "5% Yield Vault" with one swipe.
- Overlay: Green glowing text: "5.1% APY (Automated)".
- Transition: Pull back to actor smiling.

**Voiceover**:
That's why I moved my savings to an automated yield account.
It earns over five percent interest automatically.
With zero risk and zero fees.

---

## Scene 4 — Proof (22-30s)

**Goal**: Show the concrete dollar difference.
**Emotion**: Greed / Desire.
**Viewer Thought**: "That's free money I'm missing out on."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Split screen graphic.
- Expression: N/A.
- Action: Left side shows "Old Bank: $1.20/yr". Right side shows "New App: $500/yr" with a green number ticking upward.
- Overlay: None (let the numbers dominate).
- Transition: Match cut to actor.

**Voiceover**:
On ten thousand dollars...
My old bank paid me a dollar a year.
This account pays me five hundred dollars.
For doing literally nothing.

---

## Scene 5 — Future Pacing & CTA (30-40s)

**Goal**: Drive immediate downloads.
**Emotion**: Urgency (Loss Aversion).
**Viewer Thought**: "I'm moving my money right now."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot. Bright, lifestyle setting.
- Expression: Direct, friendly eye contact.
- Action: Pointing down to the link area.
- Overlay: UI Graphic: "Open Account in 2 Minutes".
- Transition: Fade to black.

**Voiceover**:
Stop letting your bank get rich off your savings.
Click the link below to open an account in two minutes.
