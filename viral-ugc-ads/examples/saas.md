# Example Script: B2B SaaS

**Product**: AI Bookkeeping Software for Agencies
**Audience**: Agency Owners and Freelancers
**Duration**: 55s

---

## Scene 1 — Pattern Interrupt (0-3s)

**Goal**: Stop the scroll with a controversial statement about their time.
**Emotion**: Defensive / Intrigued.
**Viewer Thought**: "Is he talking to me?"

**Visual**:
- Camera: Low angle Medium Shot. Harsh cinematic lighting.
- Expression: Deadpan, slightly exhausted.
- Action: Actor drops a massive stack of crumpled receipts onto a clean desk.
- Overlay: Big bold text: "AGENCY OWNERS:"
- Transition: Smash zoom into the receipts.

**Voiceover**:
If you're a serious founder...

---

## Scene 2 — The Call Out (3-10s)

**Goal**: Agitate the pain of manual admin work.
**Emotion**: Frustration.
**Viewer Thought**: "I hate doing this every month."

**Visual**:
- Camera: High angle, looking down at a laptop keyboard and coffee cup.
- Expression: Unseen.
- Action: Fast, aggressive typing. Switching between 4 different Excel tabs.
- Overlay: Text bubble: "Where did that $400 go?"
- Transition: Quick cut to actor rubbing temples.

**Voiceover**:
You shouldn't be doing your own bookkeeping.
Spending your Sundays chasing down invoices...
And fighting with messy spreadsheets.

---

## Scene 3 — The Enemy (10-18s)

**Goal**: Shift blame to traditional tools.
**Emotion**: Validation.
**Viewer Thought**: "Exactly. Quickbooks is too complicated."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, handheld (slight shake). Warm office lighting.
- Expression: Intense, explaining.
- Action: Gesturing with hands, pointing off-screen.
- Overlay: Red text: "Burning Cash."
- Transition: Whip pan left.

**Voiceover**:
Traditional software is built for accountants.
Not for agency owners.
It's complicated, it's slow, and it's burning your cash.

---

## Scene 4 — Mechanism Reveal (18-28s)

**Goal**: Introduce the AI automation as a magic pill.
**Emotion**: Relief.
**Viewer Thought**: "Wait, it does it automatically?"

**Visual**:
- Camera: Screen push into the software UI.
- Expression: N/A
- Action: A cursor clicks one button labeled "Auto-Reconcile." The screen instantly categorizes 50 transactions with green checkmarks.
- Overlay: Green progress bar fills instantly.
- Transition: Smooth pull out back to actor.

**Voiceover**:
That's why agencies are switching to AI.
You just connect your accounts once.
And it categorizes every transaction automatically.

---

## Scene 5 — Proof (28-38s)

**Goal**: Prove it's accurate and trusted.
**Emotion**: Trust.
**Viewer Thought**: "Okay, it actually works."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, actor walking and talking in a nice office.
- Expression: Confident, smiling.
- Action: Holding phone up, displaying a clean P&L dashboard.
- Overlay: Three 5-star review popups from "Verified Agency Owners" appear quickly.
- Transition: Match cut.

**Voiceover**:
No manual data entry.
Zero math mistakes.
Just a perfect P&L statement, ready for tax season.

---

## Scene 6 — Future Pacing (38-48s)

**Goal**: Paint the picture of getting time back.
**Emotion**: Aspirational.
**Viewer Thought**: "I want my weekends back."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot. Sunny, outdoor café setting.
- Expression: Relaxed, enjoying a coffee.
- Action: Closing a laptop with one hand, picking up the coffee.
- Overlay: Text: "Get your weekends back."
- Transition: Slow push in.

**Voiceover**:
Imagine closing your books for the month...
In under five minutes.
So you can actually focus on closing clients.

---

## Scene 7 — CTA (48-55s)

**Goal**: Low-friction next step.
**Emotion**: Urgency.
**Viewer Thought**: "I'll try the free trial."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, locked off.
- Expression: Direct eye contact, inviting.
- Action: Pointing down.
- Overlay: UI Graphic: "Start 14-Day Free Trial."
- Transition: Fade out.

**Voiceover**:
Stop fighting with spreadsheets.
Click below to start your free trial today.
