# Scene System & Metadata

Every scene in the final output MUST use this exact formatting template. Do not deviate.

## Required Format

```markdown
---
## Scene [Number] — [Scene Type/Role] ([Duration]s)

### Psychological Strategy
- **Goal:** [Why this scene exists in the funnel (e.g., Agitate pain, Prove authority)]
- **Target Emotion:** [e.g., Frustration, Awe, Relief]
- **Viewer Inner Thought:** ["What the viewer should be thinking in this exact moment"]

### Visual Direction (Google Flow Optimized)
- **Shot:** [e.g., Close Up (CU)]
- **Lens:** [e.g., 35mm]
- **Lighting:** [e.g., Dark room, screen glow]
- **Environment:** [e.g., Messy home office desk]
- **Camera Motion:** [e.g., Handheld slight shake -> Snap Zoom at 0:02]
- **Depth of Field:** [e.g., Shallow]
- **Facial Expression:** [e.g., Exhausted, squinting]
- **Overlay:** [e.g., Red declining graph graphic]
- **Subtitle:** [e.g., Bold yellow text]
- **Sound Cue:** [e.g., SFX: Glitch sound]
- **Transition:** [e.g., Whip pan right]

### Voiceover
**Tone:** [e.g., The Empath (Soft, tired)]
[Line 1 - Max 10 words]
[Line 2 - Max 10 words]
[Line 3 - Max 10 words]
```

## Scene Types
(Use these labels for the `[Scene Type/Role]` bracket)
- **Pattern Interrupt** (0-3s)
- **The Call Out** (3-8s)
- **The Enemy / Root Cause** (8-15s)
- **Mechanism Reveal** (15-22s)
- **Demonstration** (15-25s)
- **Irrefutable Proof** (22-35s)
- **Future Pacing** (35-45s)
- **The CTA** (45-60s)
