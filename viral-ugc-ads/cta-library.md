# CTA Library (Calls to Action)

The CTA is the final 5-10 seconds of the video. It must be low-friction, clear, and logically connected to the value demonstrated in the video.

## The "Value First" CTA (Best for Lead Gen / Ebooks / Webinars)
- "I put all 100 templates into a free doc. Link is in my bio."
- "If you want the exact blueprint I used, grab it below."
- "I made a free training breaking down the exact math. Go watch it."
- "Stop guessing. Get the cheat sheet in my bio."
- "I saved the exact prompt. You can copy it from the link in my profile."

## The "Curiosity" CTA (Best for Traffic / Tools / SaaS)
- "Search for [Brand Name] and see for yourself."
- "Click the link to see how much you're overpaying."
- "Try the free calculator in my bio to see your actual numbers."
- "Want to see how it works on your account? Link below."
- "Go see what your score is right now."

## The "Direct" CTA (Best for E-commerce / Physical Products)
- "They always sell out, so grab yours before they're gone."
- "Click the link below to get 20% off your first order."
- "Stop struggling with [Pain]. Get yours today."
- "Link is in the description to upgrade your [Setup/Routine]."
- "Treat yourself. Click below."

## The "Community/Engagement" CTA (Best for Organic TikTok/Reels)
- "Save this video so you don't forget the prompt."
- "Send this to a founder who needs to hear it."
- "Comment 'READY' and I'll DM you the link."
- "What do you think? Am I crazy? Let me know in the comments."
- "Hit follow if you want to scale your [Skill/Business]."

## Visual Rules for the CTA
1. **Always Point**: The actor must visually point down (for TikTok/Reels descriptions) or toward the screen (for YouTube end screens).
2. **UI Overlays**: Use a graphic overlay showing a cursor clicking a "Link in Bio" button or a search bar typing the brand name.
3. **Keep it Clean**: Remove distracting background elements. The visual focus must be entirely on the action you want them to take.
