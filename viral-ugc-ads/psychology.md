# Psychological Triggers

Direct response scripts work because they trigger specific evolutionary and psychological responses. Apply these triggers intentionally based on the product and audience.

## 1. The Curiosity Gap (The Open Loop)
Create a gap between what the viewer knows and what they want to know.
- **How to use it**: Withhold the core mechanism until Scene 4. Tease it in Scene 1 and 2.
- **Example VO**: "There's a weird setting in iOS 17 that nobody is talking about..."
- **Visual implementation**: Show the result of the setting, blur the actual toggle switch until the reveal.

## 2. Loss Aversion
People are twice as motivated to avoid losing something as they are to gain something.
- **How to use it**: Frame the problem not as "missing out on growth" but as "leaking money/time".
- **Example VO**: "You are actively losing 40% of your leads because your form looks like this."
- **Visual implementation**: Red arrows pointing down, leaking bucket metaphors, money counter ticking backwards.

## 3. The Contrarian / Enemy Creation
Unite the viewer and the creator against a common enemy (a mainstream belief, a competitor, an old way of doing things).
- **How to use it**: Tell them everything they've been taught is wrong. It absolves them of guilt for past failures.
- **Example VO**: "Stop waking up at 5 AM. Hustle culture is killing your productivity."
- **Visual implementation**: Crossing out or throwing away traditional symbols (alarm clocks, planners).

## 4. Status Affirmation / Identity
People buy things that align with who they believe they are (or want to be).
- **How to use it**: Use identity labels. Don't say "people who make videos," say "Creators." Don't say "business owners," say "Founders."
- **Example VO**: "If you're a serious founder, you shouldn't be doing your own bookkeeping."
- **Visual implementation**: Surround the actor with status symbols relevant to that niche (high-end espresso machine, clean minimal desk, specific books).

## 5. Simplicity / The Magic Pill
People want the maximum result with the minimum required effort.
- **How to use it**: Emphasize how much work the mechanism removes. Use words like "done for you," "copy paste," "plug and play."
- **Example VO**: "I just copy and paste this one prompt, and it does a week's worth of research in 4 seconds."
- **Visual implementation**: Finger snapping, fast forward time-lapses, loading bars instantly jumping to 100%.

## 6. Social Proof & Bandwagon Effect
People look to others to determine correct behavior.
- **How to use it**: Show, don't tell, that many others are succeeding with this.
- **Example VO**: "This is why 10,000 creators switched to this platform last month."
- **Visual implementation**: Rapid montage of 5-star reviews, notification spam (sales/followers coming in fast), large community numbers.

## 7. Future Pacing
Mentally transporting the prospect to a future where their problem is solved.
- **How to use it**: Describe a specific, sensory detail of their improved life.
- **Example VO**: "Imagine waking up, checking your Stripe dashboard, and seeing your rent is already paid by 9 AM."
- **Visual implementation**: Waking up smiling, checking phone in bed, relaxed posture, bright warm lighting.

## Selecting the Right Trigger
- **B2B / SaaS**: Prioritize Loss Aversion (time/money) and Simplicity.
- **Info Products / Courses**: Prioritize The Contrarian and Future Pacing.
- **Physical Products**: Prioritize Demonstration (Simplicity) and Social Proof.
