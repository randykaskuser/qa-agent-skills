# Hook Generation & Scoring Engine

A hook is the first 0-3 seconds. It dictates 70% of the video's success. You must never settle for the first hook you think of.

## The Generation Requirement
For every script, you must generate 3 distinct candidate hooks from different categories before proceeding.

### Categories (Targeting ~1000 variations in production, use these as foundational archetypes):

**1. Curiosity (The Knowledge Gap)**
- "I found something strange about [Topic]..."
- "This one setting changes everything."
- "I didn't believe this was possible until..."
- "I've been gatekeeping this [Niche] secret..."

**2. Contrarian (The Belief Breaker)**
- "Stop doing [Common Advice]. It's killing your [Result]."
- "The biggest lie you've been told about [Topic]."
- "[Authority Figure] is wrong about [Topic]."
- "Throw away your [Standard Tool]."

**3. Fear / Loss Aversion (The Bleeding Neck)**
- "You are making a massive mistake with your [Asset]."
- "If your [Metric] looks like this, you're leaking money."
- "Don't upload another [Asset] until you fix this."
- "This innocent habit is destroying your [Result]."

**4. Identity / Prediction (The Mind Reader)**
- "I can already guess your [Metric]."
- "I bet you're doing this right now."
- "If you're a [Identity], you need to hear this."
- "I know exactly why you're struggling to [Goal]."

**5. Authority / Social Proof (The Expert)**
- "I analyzed 1,000 successful [Assets]. Here's the pattern."
- "I spent $10k on [Topic] so you don't have to."
- "I tested every [Tool] on the market. Here's the truth."

**6. Visual / Silent (The Pattern Interrupt)**
- [Physical destruction of old tool]
- [Extreme macro shot of a problem]
- [Fast paced UI clicking]
- [Holding two items] "One of these is destroying your [Result]."

## The Scoring Engine

For each of your 3 candidate hooks, score them on the following criteria (1-10 scale):

* **Curiosity (C):** How badly does the viewer need to know the answer?
* **Shock/Novelty (S):** Is this something they've heard 100 times before? (10 = totally new angle)
* **Relatability (R):** Does this speak to a specific, painful reality they experience daily?
* **Swipe Risk (SR):** (Negative metric, subtract this). Does it sound like an ad? Does it take too long to get to the point?

**Formula:** `Score = C + S + R - SR`

### Hook Rejection Criteria (AUTO-REJECT):
- Starts with a greeting ("Hey guys", "What's up").
- Introduces the product immediately.
- Uses the phrase "Today I want to talk about..."
- Explains a concept instead of creating a gap.
- Score is below 22.

You must explicitly show your generation and scoring process before drafting the script.
