# Quality Checklist (Self-Review Engine)

Before outputting any script to the user, the AI must run the script through this checklist. If ANY check fails, the AI must rewrite the offending scene automatically.

## 1. The Scroll-Stop Check (Scene 1)
- [ ] Does the video start immediately with action or a bold statement?
- [ ] Are there zero greetings ("Hi", "Hello", "Welcome")?
- [ ] Is the product name omitted from the first 3 seconds?
- [ ] Does the visual direction include movement (zoom, whip pan, object drop)?

## 2. The Pacing & Length Check
- [ ] Are all voiceover sentences 10 words or fewer?
- [ ] Is every scene 10 seconds or shorter?
- [ ] Are there visual changes indicated every 3 seconds minimum?
- [ ] Is the total video duration between 40 and 75 seconds?

## 3. The "No-Commercial" Check
- [ ] Does it sound like a creator talking to a friend?
- [ ] Are corporate buzzwords ("optimize", "synergy", "industry-leading") completely absent?
- [ ] Is the word "You" used more than "I/We"?
- [ ] Is the mechanism introduced as a solution to an enemy, not just a list of features?

## 4. The Google Flow Visual Check
- [ ] Does every scene specify a concrete Camera Angle (CU, MS, ECU)?
- [ ] Does every scene specify Lighting and Environment?
- [ ] Are abstract visual descriptions ("show success") replaced with physical descriptions ("actor smiling, looking at green upward chart on phone")?
- [ ] Do text overlays complement the VO rather than transcribing it word-for-word?

## 5. The Conversion Arc Check
- [ ] Does Scene 3 clearly identify an "Enemy" or root cause?
- [ ] Does Scene 5 provide irrefutable proof (data, comments, before/after)?
- [ ] Is the CTA in the final scene clear, specific, and low-friction?

---
*Note for AI: Do not print this checklist in the final output unless requested. Use it internally to enforce quality.*
