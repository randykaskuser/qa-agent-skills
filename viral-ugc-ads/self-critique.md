# AI Self-Critique Engine

Before outputting the final script, you MUST perform a rigorous self-critique. You are acting as a strict Creative Director reviewing a junior copywriter's draft.

If any section scores below 8/10, you MUST rewrite it immediately before presenting the final output.

## Grading Rubric

### 1. Hook (Score: X/10)
- Does it stop the scroll in under 2 seconds?
- Is it free of greetings ("Hey guys") and product names?
- Does it create an immediate curiosity gap or challenge a belief?
- *Reason for score:*
- *Rewrite needed? (Yes/No)*

### 2. Retention / Pacing (Score: X/10)
- Is the 2.5-second cinematic rule enforced? (Are there constant visual changes/overlays/motions?)
- Is Scene 3 (The Enemy) too slow or explanatory?
- Does the script drag before revealing the mechanism?
- *Reason for score:*
- *Rewrite needed? (Yes/No)*

### 3. Visual & Cinematic Direction (Score: X/10)
- Are the Google Flow tags granular and physical? (Shot, Lens, Lighting, Motion, DOF, etc.)
- Are there abstract instructions ("Show success") that need to be physicalized ("Show user smiling at green upward chart")?
- *Reason for score:*
- *Rewrite needed? (Yes/No)*

### 4. Voiceover & Tone (Score: X/10)
- Are all lines 10 words or fewer?
- Does it sound like a creator (not a corporate ad)?
- Are emotional tone tags (Authority, Empath, Excitable) utilized properly?
- *Reason for score:*
- *Rewrite needed? (Yes/No)*

### 5. CTA (Score: X/10)
- Is it low-friction?
- Does it logically connect to the value shown in the video?
- Is it overly salesy or desperate?
- *Reason for score:*
- *Rewrite needed? (Yes/No)*

---

## Output Format for Self-Critique

You must explicitly output your critique before the final script, like this:

```markdown
## Creative Director Review

**Hook:** 8/10 - Strong pattern interrupt, but the VO was slightly too long. Rewritten for punchiness.
**Retention:** 7/10 - Scene 3 dragged. I added a snap zoom and a UI overlay to increase visual energy. Rewritten.
**Visual:** 9/10 - Google Flow tags are precise and physical.
**Voice:** 8/10 - Eliminated 3 filler words ("basically", "just"). Sentences are short.
**CTA:** 9/10 - Clear, direct, no fake urgency.

*(If rewrites were needed, apply them to the final script below)*
```
