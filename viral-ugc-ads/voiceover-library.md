# Voiceover & Emotion Library

The voiceover must sound like a human creator, not a brand. The emotional tone must shift throughout the video.

## Emotion Tags

You must tag every scene's voiceover with a specific emotional delivery style.

### 1. The Authority (Confident, direct, slightly aggressive)
Use for: The Call Out, The Enemy.
- "Stop doing this right now."
- "You are actively losing money."
- "Everyone telling you this is lying."

### 2. The Secret Sharer (Soft, conspiratorial, close to mic)
Use for: The Hook, The Mechanism Reveal.
- "I probably shouldn't show you this, but..."
- "There is a hidden setting nobody talks about."
- "I've been gatekeeping this for three years."

### 3. The Empath (Warm, understanding, slightly defeated)
Use for: Pain Agitation, The Call Out.
- "I know how exhausting it is."
- "You spend hours editing, just for 12 views."
- "It feels like you're doing everything right, but nothing works."

### 4. The Excitable Discoverer (High energy, fast-paced, amazed)
Use for: The Demonstration, The Proof.
- "Look at this! It literally does it for you in two seconds."
- "My mind was completely blown."
- "I couldn't believe it actually worked this fast."

### 5. The Relieved Guide (Calm, aspirational, slow paced)
Use for: Future Pacing.
- "Imagine just waking up and it's already done."
- "I finally got my weekends back."
- "You can actually focus on what matters."

### 6. The Urgent Instructor (Firm, clear, fast)
Use for: The CTA.
- "Don't wait on this. Click the link right now."
- "They always sell out fast, so grab yours today."
- "Stop guessing. Get the blueprint below."

## Copywriting Constraints
- **Max 10 words per line.** Break lines at natural breathing pauses.
- **No Filler.** Delete: basically, actually, literally, very, really, just, so, well.
- **Active Voice.** "This tool saves you time" NOT "Time is saved by this tool."
- **Use Ellipses (...)** to indicate deliberate pacing pauses.
- **Use ALL CAPS** to indicate verbal emphasis on a specific word.
