# Example Script: Physical Product (Gadget)

**Product**: Magnetic Wireless Power Bank (MagSafe style)
**Audience**: Travelers, commuters, people constantly on their phones
**Duration**: 35s

---

## Scene 1 — Pattern Interrupt (0-3s)

**Goal**: Stop the scroll by showing a highly relatable, frustrating moment.
**Emotion**: Frustration.
**Viewer Thought**: "I hate when that happens."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Extreme Close Up (ECU), low angle.
- Expression: Unseen.
- Action: A tangled mess of white charging cables falls aggressively onto a desk.
- Overlay: Big bold text: "1% BATTERY."
- Transition: Fast whip pan up to actor's face.

**Voiceover**:
Stop carrying these around everywhere you go.

---

## Scene 2 — The Call Out & Enemy (3-10s)

**Goal**: Agitate the pain of traditional bulky power banks and cables.
**Emotion**: Annoyance.
**Viewer Thought**: "My power bank is a brick."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Over the shoulder (OTS).
- Expression: Annoyed.
- Action: Trying to stuff a massive, brick-like power bank and a long cable into a small pocket or purse. It doesn't fit.
- Overlay: Red X Graphic over the pocket.
- Transition: Snap zoom into the actor's face.

**Voiceover**:
Normal power banks are completely useless.
They're the size of a brick.
And you always forget the cable anyway.

---

## Scene 3 — Mechanism Reveal & Demo (10-18s)

**Goal**: Visually demonstrate the satisfying, frictionless solution.
**Emotion**: Relief / Satisfaction.
**Viewer Thought**: "That click is so satisfying."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up macro shot, locked off.
- Expression: N/A.
- Action: A sleek, thin magnetic battery snaps satisfyingly onto the back of a phone. The screen lights up with the charging symbol immediately.
- Overlay: Green battery charging animation.
- Transition: Cut to actor holding the phone easily in one hand.

**Voiceover**:
This is a magnetic wireless power bank.
It snaps directly to the back of your phone.
No cables. No bulk.

---

## Scene 4 — Proof & Lifestyle (18-28s)

**Goal**: Show how it fits effortlessly into their life.
**Emotion**: Desire.
**Viewer Thought**: "I need this for my next trip."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Fast-paced lifestyle montage (1 second per shot).
- Expression: Relaxed, unbothered.
- Action:
  1. Slipping the phone (with battery attached) effortlessly into tight jeans.
  2. Holding the phone up to take a selfie (showing how thin it is).
  3. Phone sitting on a cafe table charging cleanly.
- Overlay: Text tracking: "Ultra-Thin", "Fast Charging".
- Transition: Match cut back to actor at desk.

**Voiceover**:
It's so thin you forget it's there.
It holds two full charges.
And it actually fits in your pocket.

---

## Scene 5 — CTA (28-35s)

**Goal**: Drive an impulse purchase based on convenience.
**Emotion**: Urgency.
**Viewer Thought**: "I'm buying one right now."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot. Clean, bright lighting.
- Expression: Direct, friendly eye contact.
- Action: Holding the product, snapping it onto the phone one last time, pointing down.
- Overlay: Graphic: "Shop Now (Free Shipping)".
- Transition: Fade to black.

**Voiceover**:
Never deal with a dead phone again.
Click the link below to grab yours before they sell out.
