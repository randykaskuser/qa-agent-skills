# Example Script: Digital Course (High Ticket)

**Product**: "YouTube Channel Launchpad" Course
**Audience**: Aspiring YouTubers who are afraid to start or failing to grow
**Duration**: 60s

---

## Scene 1 — Pattern Interrupt (0-4s)

**Goal**: Stop the scroll by challenging the biggest misconception.
**Emotion**: Surprise.
**Viewer Thought**: "Wait, really? But everyone says I need a good camera."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Extreme Close Up (ECU), slightly high angle. 
- Expression: Serious, leaning into the lens.
- Action: Actor holds up an expensive DSLR camera, then physically pushes it out of the frame, bringing up a standard iPhone.
- Overlay: Bold text: "STOP BUYING GEAR."
- Transition: Fast snap zoom out to medium shot.

**Voiceover**:
Stop buying expensive cameras.
Nobody cares about your 4K footage.

---

## Scene 2 — The Call Out (4-12s)

**Goal**: Identify their exact frustrating situation.
**Emotion**: Empathy / Frustration.
**Viewer Thought**: "I spend so much time editing for nothing."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Over the shoulder (OTS). Dark, moody room illuminated only by monitor glow.
- Expression: Unseen. Posture is defeated.
- Action: Watching a YouTube video on their own channel sitting at 14 views.
- Overlay: Text bubble popping up: "14 Views (10 are you)".
- Transition: Whip pan to actor looking directly at camera.

**Voiceover**:
If you're spending ten hours editing a video...
Just to get fourteen views.
You are focusing on the wrong things.

---

## Scene 3 — The Enemy (12-20s)

**Goal**: Shift blame from their talent to their strategy.
**Emotion**: Epiphany.
**Viewer Thought**: "So my content isn't bad, my packaging is."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, dynamic handheld movement.
- Expression: Intense, explaining like a mentor.
- Action: Pointing aggressively at the screen.
- Overlay: Split screen showing a boring thumbnail vs a highly clickable one.
- Transition: Crash zoom into the clickable thumbnail.

**Voiceover**:
It's not because your content is bad.
It's because nobody is clicking your thumbnails.
And your first ten seconds are boring.

---

## Scene 4 — Mechanism Reveal (20-32s)

**Goal**: Introduce the specific framework taught in the course.
**Emotion**: Curiosity / Hope.
**Viewer Thought**: "I need that framework."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Screen push into a Notion document or slide deck.
- Expression: N/A.
- Action: Fast scroll through a document titled "The 3-Part Viral Hook Formula."
- Overlay: Progress bar filling up instantly.
- Transition: Cut back to actor, confident smile.

**Voiceover**:
You don't need better gear.
You need a retention strategy.
I use a three-step formula for every single video.
Hook, Retain, Payoff.

---

## Scene 5 — Proof (32-42s)

**Goal**: Prove the authority of the creator.
**Emotion**: Trust / Awe.
**Viewer Thought**: "Okay, they know what they're talking about."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Fast-paced montage (1 second per shot).
- Expression: Confident, pointing at data.
- Action: Show YouTube Studio dashboard with a green line shooting upwards.
- Overlay: Rapidly increasing subscriber counter graphic.
- Transition: Match cut to actor at desk.

**Voiceover**:
I used this exact system to go from zero...
To a hundred thousand subscribers in six months.
Without buying a single ad.

---

## Scene 6 — Future Pacing (42-52s)

**Goal**: Paint the picture of a successful YouTube career.
**Emotion**: Aspirational.
**Viewer Thought**: "I want to do this full time."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot. Sunny, aesthetic cafe.
- Expression: Relaxed, happy.
- Action: Closing a laptop, taking a sip of coffee, smiling at the camera.
- Overlay: Text: "Quit the 9-to-5."
- Transition: Slow push in.

**Voiceover**:
Imagine finally monetizing your channel.
Getting brand deals in your inbox.
And actually building a full-time income from your bedroom.

---

## Scene 7 — CTA (52-60s)

**Goal**: Drive to the landing page or free training.
**Emotion**: Urgency (Internal).
**Viewer Thought**: "I'm signing up right now."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, locked off.
- Expression: Direct, inviting eye contact.
- Action: Pointing down to the description.
- Overlay: UI Graphic: "Watch Free Training."
- Transition: Fade to black.

**Voiceover**:
I broke down the entire system in a free training.
Click the link below to watch it right now.
