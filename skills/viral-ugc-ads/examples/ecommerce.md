# Example Script: E-commerce (Physical Product)

**Product**: Posture-Correcting Desk Chair Cushion
**Audience**: Remote workers with back pain
**Duration**: 45s

---

## Scene 1 — Pattern Interrupt (0-3s)

**Goal**: Shock the viewer visually with a painful relatable action.
**Emotion**: Empathy / Wincing.
**Viewer Thought**: "Ouch, I do that."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Extreme close-up of a hand aggressively massaging a lower back.
- Expression: Unseen, but body language is stiff.
- Action: Actor stands up from a desk chair and visibly winces, stretching backward.
- Overlay: Big bold text: "WORKING FROM HOME?"
- Transition: Fast snap zoom out.

**Voiceover**:
Stop blaming your mattress.

---

## Scene 2 — The Call Out (3-10s)

**Goal**: Identify the specific daily pain.
**Emotion**: Frustration.
**Viewer Thought**: "By 3 PM, my back is destroyed."

**Visual**:
- Camera: High angle, looking down at actor hunched over a laptop.
- Expression: Strained, squinting at screen.
- Action: Slouching deeper into a standard, expensive-looking office chair.
- Overlay: Text bubble: "3:00 PM."
- Transition: Quick cut to close up of actor's face.

**Voiceover**:
If your lower back is on fire by 3 PM...
It's because of how you're sitting right now.

---

## Scene 3 — The Enemy (10-18s)

**Goal**: Shift blame from the user to their "ergonomic" chair.
**Emotion**: Validation.
**Viewer Thought**: "But I spent $300 on this chair!"

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot, handheld.
- Expression: Skeptical.
- Action: Actor slaps the seat of a standard mesh office chair.
- Overlay: Red X graphic over the chair.
- Transition: Whip pan down.

**Voiceover**:
Those expensive ergonomic chairs?
They don't actually support your pelvis.
They just force your spine into a weird curve.

---

## Scene 4 — Mechanism Reveal & Demo (18-28s)

**Goal**: Visually demonstrate the solution working instantly.
**Emotion**: Relief / Desire.
**Viewer Thought**: "That looks so comfortable."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot, locked off. Clean, bright room.
- Expression: Immediate relief, sighing happily.
- Action: Actor places the black memory-foam cushion on the chair, sits down, and their spine visibly straightens effortlessly.
- Overlay: An animated neon green line showing perfect spinal alignment.
- Transition: Push in to the cushion material.

**Voiceover**:
That's why I switched to orthopedic memory foam.
It actively tilts your pelvis forward.
Forcing perfect posture without you even trying.

---

## Scene 5 — Proof (28-35s)

**Goal**: Prove the physical quality and results.
**Emotion**: Trust.
**Viewer Thought**: "It looks high quality."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Extreme close-up (Macro shot).
- Expression: N/A.
- Action: A hand presses hard into the foam, and it slowly, satisfyingly rises back up.
- Overlay: Star rating graphic (4.9/5) pops up.
- Transition: Match cut to actor at desk.

**Voiceover**:
It never flattens out.
And my lower back pain?
Completely gone in three days.

---

## Scene 6 — Future Pacing (35-40s)

**Goal**: Emotional payoff.
**Emotion**: Aspirational.
**Viewer Thought**: "I want to finish work without pain."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot. Golden hour lighting coming through window.
- Expression: Energetic, smiling.
- Action: Actor finishes work, stands up smoothly without wincing, and grabs a gym bag.
- Overlay: None.
- Transition: Fast push in.

**Voiceover**:
Imagine finishing an eight-hour shift...
And actually having the energy to go to the gym.

---

## Scene 7 — CTA (40-45s)

**Goal**: Drive impulse purchase.
**Emotion**: Urgency.
**Viewer Thought**: "I need this now."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, direct to camera.
- Expression: Urgent but friendly.
- Action: Holding the cushion, pointing to the bottom of the screen.
- Overlay: Text: "50% OFF TODAY."
- Transition: Fade out.

**Voiceover**:
They always sell out on TikTok.
Grab yours before they're gone again.
