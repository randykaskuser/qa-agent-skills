# Example Script: Digital Product (Ebook)

**Product**: "160 Viral Hook Templates" Ebook
**Audience**: Content Creators struggling with views
**Duration**: 45s

---

## Scene 1 — Pattern Interrupt (0-3s)

**Goal**: Stop the scroll by challenging a core belief.
**Emotion**: Defensive curiosity.
**Viewer Thought**: "Wait, what am I doing wrong?"

**Visual**:
- Camera: Extreme Close Up (ECU), slightly low angle. Ring light reflection in eyes.
- Expression: Serious, intense eye contact.
- Action: Actor holds up a phone showing a TikTok with 12 views, then throws it out of frame.
- Overlay: Big bold text: "STOP UPLOADING."
- Transition: Fast snap zoom into actor's face.

**Voiceover**:
I can already guess your views.

---

## Scene 2 — The Call Out (3-8s)

**Goal**: Prove we understand their exact pain.
**Emotion**: Frustration/Relief that someone gets it.
**Viewer Thought**: "That's exactly what my dashboard looks like."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Over the shoulder (OTS) shot looking at a laptop screen. Dark room.
- Expression: Unseen (back of head), but posture is slumped.
- Action: Mouse cursor hovering over the 'Post' button.
- Overlay: A retention graph plummeting to 0% at 2 seconds.
- Transition: Match cut to actor sitting at desk, medium shot.

**Voiceover**:
You spend three hours editing.
You finally post it.
And it dies at 200 views.

---

## Scene 3 — The Enemy (8-15s)

**Goal**: Shift the blame from them to their hooks.
**Emotion**: Epiphany.
**Viewer Thought**: "Oh, so it's not the algorithm."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, dynamic handheld movement.
- Expression: Explaining passionately, hands gesturing.
- Action: Actor points to the left side of the screen.
- Overlay: Text pops up: "It's NOT the algorithm."
- Transition: Whip pan right.

**Voiceover**:
It's not the algorithm shadowbanning you.
It's because your hooks are completely predictable.
You're losing them in the first two seconds.

---

## Scene 4 — Mechanism Reveal (15-22s)

**Goal**: Introduce the framework as the solution.
**Emotion**: Hope.
**Viewer Thought**: "I need that structure."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Screen recording pushing in fast (Screen Push).
- Expression: N/A
- Action: Fast scroll through a Notion document filled with highlighted text and formulas.
- Overlay: None (let the screen recording breathe).
- Transition: Cut back to actor, Close up.

**Voiceover**:
Top creators don't guess.
They use psychological frameworks.
Like the 'Contrarian' or the 'Open Loop'.

---

## Scene 5 — Proof (22-30s)

**Goal**: Show that the templates work immediately.
**Emotion**: Desire.
**Viewer Thought**: "I want those numbers."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot, bright natural window lighting.
- Expression: Smiling, relaxed, holding phone casually.
- Action: Actor swipes through notification center on phone.
- Overlay: Three notification bubbles pop up rapidly: "10k views", "50k views", "250k views".
- Transition: Smooth push in.

**Voiceover**:
I swapped my usual intro for a framework.
And this happened in 24 hours.

---

## Scene 6 — Future Pacing & Value (30-38s)

**Goal**: Make the product feel tangible and valuable.
**Emotion**: Relief.
**Viewer Thought**: "This will save me so much time."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Fast-paced montage (1 second per shot).
- Expression: Focused but happy.
- Action: Typing on laptop -> sipping coffee -> closing laptop confidently.
- Overlay: Text tracking with actor: "160+ Templates", "Fill in the blank".
- Transition: Flash cut between shots.

**Voiceover**:
I put all 160 of these templates into one doc.
You just fill in the blanks for your niche.
Never stare at a blank screen again.

---

## Scene 7 — CTA (38-45s)

**Goal**: Drive the click smoothly.
**Emotion**: Urgency (Internal).
**Viewer Thought**: "I'm getting this right now."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot, locked off tripod.
- Expression: Confident, direct eye contact.
- Action: Actor points down to the bottom of the frame.
- Overlay: Graphic of a mouse clicking a "Link in Bio" button.
- Transition: Fade to black.

**Voiceover**:
If you want to stop guessing...
And start growing...
Grab the cheat sheet in my bio.
