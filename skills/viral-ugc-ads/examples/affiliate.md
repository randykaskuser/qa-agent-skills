# Example Script: Affiliate Marketing (Software/Tool)

**Product**: AI Video Editing Tool (e.g., OpusClip, Descript)
**Audience**: Podcasters and Content Creators looking to repurpose long-form content
**Duration**: 40s

---

## Scene 1 — Pattern Interrupt (0-4s)

**Goal**: Stop the scroll by showing an impossibly fast result.
**Emotion**: Disbelief / Curiosity.
**Viewer Thought**: "Wait, how did they do that so fast?"

**Visual**:
- Camera: Extreme Close Up (ECU) of a phone screen.
- Expression: N/A.
- Action: A finger taps one button labeled "Generate Clips". The screen instantly populates with 15 fully edited, captioned short-form videos.
- Overlay: Big bold text: "1 HOUR OF WORK IN 10 SECONDS."
- Transition: Fast snap zoom out to the actor's face.

**Voiceover**:
Watch me edit fifteen TikToks in ten seconds.

---

## Scene 2 — The Call Out & Enemy (4-12s)

**Goal**: Agitate the pain of manual editing.
**Emotion**: Frustration / Empathy.
**Viewer Thought**: "Clipping podcasts takes me all weekend."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Over the shoulder (OTS). 
- Expression: Unseen. Posture is tense.
- Action: Actor scrubbing slowly through a massive Premiere Pro timeline, looking frustrated.
- Overlay: Text bubble: "3 hours later..."
- Transition: Whip pan to actor looking directly at camera.

**Voiceover**:
If you are still manually clipping your podcast...
Finding the best moments.
Adding the captions yourself.
You are wasting hours of your life.

---

## Scene 3 — Mechanism Reveal (12-22s)

**Goal**: Show exactly how the tool works.
**Emotion**: Awe / Relief.
**Viewer Thought**: "I need this tool immediately."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Screen recording push-in.
- Expression: N/A.
- Action: Show pasting a YouTube link into the software UI. The software highlights the "viral score" of a generated clip.
- Overlay: An animated green arrow pointing to a high "Viral Score".
- Transition: Cut back to actor, Close up.

**Voiceover**:
I found an AI that does it all for you.
You just drop in a YouTube link.
It finds the most viral moments.
And adds perfect captions automatically.

---

## Scene 4 — Proof (22-30s)

**Goal**: Show the end result of using the tool.
**Emotion**: Desire.
**Viewer Thought**: "Those look like professional edits."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Split screen.
- Expression: N/A.
- Action: Three vertical videos playing simultaneously on screen. They have engaging, colorful, animated captions (Hormozi style).
- Overlay: None (let the videos speak for themselves).
- Transition: Match cut to actor at desk.

**Voiceover**:
The edits look exactly like the top creators.
With the emojis, b-roll, and highlighted text.
Without hiring a thousand-dollar editor.

---

## Scene 5 — Future Pacing & CTA (30-40s)

**Goal**: Paint the picture of a massive output increase and drive the click.
**Emotion**: Urgency / Excitement.
**Viewer Thought**: "I'm trying this right now."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot. Bright, energetic lighting.
- Expression: Confident, pointing at the camera.
- Action: Actor points to the bottom left of the screen.
- Overlay: Graphic of a cursor clicking a "Try Free" button.
- Transition: Fade to black.

**Voiceover**:
You can literally post three times a day now.
With zero extra effort.
Click the link in my bio to try it for free.
