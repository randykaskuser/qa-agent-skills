# Example Script: High-Ticket Coaching

**Product**: Fitness/Transformation Coaching Program
**Audience**: Busy professionals who have tried every diet and failed
**Duration**: 50s

---

## Scene 1 — Pattern Interrupt (0-4s)

**Goal**: Stop the scroll by attacking a mainstream belief.
**Emotion**: Defensive curiosity.
**Viewer Thought**: "But my trainer told me I have to do cardio."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Low angle close up. Gym environment.
- Expression: Serious, confident.
- Action: Actor steps off a treadmill and throws a towel over the display.
- Overlay: Bold red text: "CARDIO IS MAKING YOU FAT."
- Transition: Whip pan to actor standing in the weight room.

**Voiceover**:
Stop doing endless hours of cardio.
It is actively destroying your metabolism.

---

## Scene 2 — The Call Out (4-12s)

**Goal**: Identify the cycle of failure.
**Emotion**: Frustration / Empathy.
**Viewer Thought**: "That's exactly what happens to me every January."

**Visual**:
- Camera: High angle, looking down at a scale.
- Expression: Unseen.
- Action: Actor steps on the scale, shakes head.
- Overlay: A line graph showing weight dropping then spiking back up (yo-yo dieting).
- Transition: Fast cut to actor in the kitchen.

**Voiceover**:
You starve yourself all week.
You run three miles a day.
You lose five pounds, then gain back ten on the weekend.

---

## Scene 3 — The Enemy (12-20s)

**Goal**: Shift blame from their lack of willpower to the fitness industry.
**Emotion**: Relief / Anger at the industry.
**Viewer Thought**: "So it's not my fault."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, dynamic handheld.
- Expression: Passionate, explaining.
- Action: Actor throws a generic "detox tea" box into the trash.
- Overlay: Text: "The Fitness Industry is Lying."
- Transition: Crash zoom into actor's eyes.

**Voiceover**:
It is not your fault you can't keep the weight off.
The fitness industry wants you stuck in a loop.
Selling you temporary diets that wreck your hormones.

---

## Scene 4 — Mechanism Reveal (20-30s)

**Goal**: Introduce the unique coaching methodology.
**Emotion**: Hope.
**Viewer Thought**: "What's the permanent fix?"

**Visual**:
- Camera: Medium shot, clean bright lighting.
- Expression: Calm, authoritative.
- Action: Actor gestures to a simple whiteboard drawing showing muscle mass increasing metabolism.
- Overlay: Text: "Metabolic Reconstruction."
- Transition: Cut to actor holding a plate of actual food (steak, potatoes).

**Voiceover**:
You don't need a diet. You need Metabolic Reconstruction.
We focus entirely on building lean tissue.
So you can actually eat real food.
And burn fat while sitting at your desk.

---

## Scene 5 — Proof (30-40s)

**Goal**: Show that real, busy people achieve this.
**Emotion**: Trust / Aspiration.
**Viewer Thought**: "If that busy dad can do it, I can."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Fast montage.
- Expression: N/A.
- Action: Show 3 rapid before-and-after photos of normal-looking people (not bodybuilders).
- Overlay: Text tracking: "Lost 30lbs", "Cured back pain", "Eating 2500 calories".
- Transition: Match cut to actor.

**Voiceover**:
This is how my clients are dropping thirty pounds.
Working sixty-hour weeks.
Without giving up dinner with their kids.

---

## Scene 6 — CTA (40-50s)

**Goal**: Filter for high-intent leads and drive to a call/application.
**Emotion**: Urgency (Exclusive).
**Viewer Thought**: "I need to apply before spots fill up."

**Visual**:
- Camera: Close up, locked off.
- Expression: Direct, inviting but firm.
- Action: Pointing down.
- Overlay: Graphic of a calendar booking page.
- Transition: Fade to black.

**Voiceover**:
I am looking for five serious professionals this month.
If you're ready to fix your metabolism for good.
Click the link to apply for coaching.
