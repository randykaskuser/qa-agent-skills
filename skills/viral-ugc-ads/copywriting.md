# Copywriting Rules

## The 10-Word Rule
No sentence in the voiceover should be longer than 10 words. If it is, break it into two sentences.
- **Wrong**: "Our new software platform helps you automate your entire email marketing funnel so you can spend more time focusing on growing your actual business instead of writing emails." (28 words)
- **Right**:
  "Stop writing your own emails." (5)
  "This new software automates your entire funnel." (7)
  "So you can actually focus on growing your business." (9)

## The "You" and "I" Ratio
Use the word "You" 3x more than the words "I", "Me", or "We". The script is about the viewer, not the creator or the brand.

## Visual Syncing (Show, Don't Tell)
If the voiceover says a number, the screen must show a number. If the voiceover mentions a feeling, the actor's face must show that feeling.
Never waste voiceover explaining what the viewer can already see.
- **Wrong VO**: "As you can see on this graph, the red line is going down which means you are losing money."
- **Right VO**: "This is what your bank account looks like right now." (While showing a red downward graph).

## Removing Filler Words
Eliminate these words from scripts entirely:
- Basically
- Literally
- Actually
- Very
- Really
- Just
- So
- Well
- Today I want to talk about
- In this video

## The "Even If" Clause
Overcome objections before they are raised by appending "Even if..." to your claims.
- "Get 10,000 views on your next reel... **even if** you have 0 followers."
- "Meal prep for the whole week in 20 minutes... **even if** you don't know how to cook."

## Rhythmic Pacing
Alternate sentence lengths to create a musical rhythm.
Short. Short. Long.
- "Tired of low views?" (Short)
- "Blame the algorithm?" (Short)
- "It's actually because your hooks are entirely predictable." (Long)

## Emotional Vocabulary
Use visceral, physical words instead of corporate jargon.
- **Instead of "Optimize"**: "Squeeze every drop of juice out of..."
- **Instead of "Improve"**: "Skyrocket" or "Fix the bleeding"
- **Instead of "Inefficient"**: "Burning cash"
- **Instead of "Tired"**: "Exhausted" or "Running on fumes"
