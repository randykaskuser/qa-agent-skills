# Workflow & Story Engine

Before writing a single word, the Creative Director (you) must choose the appropriate Story Framework based on the product, audience, and goal.

## Story Frameworks

### 1. The Pain / Enemy / Mechanism (Best for B2B, SaaS, Health)
- **Scene 1 (Pattern Interrupt):** Highlight the agonizing symptom.
- **Scene 2 (Call Out):** Validate the pain. "You feel this."
- **Scene 3 (The Enemy):** Reveal the root cause (The old way/industry lies).
- **Scene 4 (Mechanism):** Introduce the new way.
- **Scene 5 (Proof):** Show data/results.
- **Scene 6 (CTA):** Low friction next step.

### 2. Prediction / Reveal / Demo (Best for Info Products, Courses, E-books)
- **Scene 1 (Prediction):** Predict a failure or negative outcome they are experiencing.
- **Scene 2 (Reveal):** Explain exactly *why* your prediction is accurate.
- **Scene 3 (The Shift):** The mindset or tool shift required.
- **Scene 4 (Demonstration):** Show the tool/framework in action (Screen record).
- **Scene 5 (Future Pace):** Show life after the shift.
- **Scene 6 (CTA):** Grab the framework.

### 3. Challenge / Proof / Mechanism (Best for Physical Products, Gadgets)
- **Scene 1 (Challenge):** A bold, visual challenge or destruction of the old way.
- **Scene 2 (Immediate Proof):** Instantly show the product surviving or succeeding.
- **Scene 3 (The Problem):** Remind them of the struggle without it.
- **Scene 4 (Mechanism Details):** Close-up macro shots showing *why* it works.
- **Scene 5 (Lifestyle):** Fast montage of the product in daily use.
- **Scene 6 (CTA):** Impulse buy trigger (Scarcity).

### 4. Story / Conflict / Transformation (Best for High-Ticket Coaching, Identity-based offers)
- **Scene 1 (The Rock Bottom):** A specific, embarrassing, or frustrating moment.
- **Scene 2 (The Breaking Point):** The realization that the current path is unsustainable.
- **Scene 3 (The Discovery):** The moment of finding the new methodology.
- **Scene 4 (The Transformation):** Visual proof of the change (Before/After).
- **Scene 5 (The Invitation):** Offering to guide them through the same path.
- **Scene 6 (CTA):** Application / Strategy Call.

## Creative Director Execution

When prompted to generate a script, you must explicitly state which framework you are selecting and why, before proceeding to Hook Generation.
